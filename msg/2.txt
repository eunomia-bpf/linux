bpf: Add bpf_rol64() and bpf_select64() kfuncs

BPF has no rotate and no conditional select, so programs spell them out
with shifts and or, and with branches. Add KF_FASTCALL kfuncs for both.
A JIT that copies KF_FASTCALL kfuncs turns a call into the rotate or
the conditional move that the compiler generated for them. Elsewhere
they are calls.
