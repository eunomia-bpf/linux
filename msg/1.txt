bpf: Let JITs inline KF_FASTCALL kfuncs

With the bpf_fastcall convention, LLVM keeps the registers R1-R5 that
are not arguments live across a call by spilling them before the call
and filling them after it. The verifier removes these spills and fills
when the call does not clobber the registers. For kfuncs it does that
for every KF_FASTCALL kfunc, which is right only because the two in the
tree, bpf_cast_to_kern_ctx() and bpf_rdonly_cast(), are replaced by
the verifier.

Let the JIT inline KF_FASTCALL kfuncs too. bpf_add_kfunc_call() asks
it with bpf_jit_inlines_kfunc_call() and sets BTF_FMODEL_JIT_INLINE in
the func_model of the kfunc if it does. A call is then treated as
bpf_fastcall only if the verifier or the JIT inlines it. Otherwise the
spills and fills stay and the kfunc is called as before.

The verifier also records which registers it removed the spills and
fills of, and bpf_fastcall_spilled_regs() tells the JIT, so that it
saves only the registers that are live after the call.
