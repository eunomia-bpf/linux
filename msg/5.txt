selftests/bpf: Test KF_FASTCALL kfuncs that the JIT inlines

Add three KF_FASTCALL kfuncs to bpf_testmod: a comparison, which
compiles to setcc on x86-64 and to cset on arm64, a function whose code
uses registers beyond its argument, and a division, which has a branch
and is therefore called.

kfunc_inline_jit checks that the verifier removes the spills and fills
around calls that the JIT inlines and keeps them around the division,
and what the x86-64 and arm64 JITs emit. It also checks that registers
that are live after a call keep their values when the copied code uses
them, and that they are not saved when they are dead. kfunc_inline
compares the results of bpf_rol64(), bpf_select64() and the bpf_testmod
kfuncs with their C definitions on random inputs.

The tests are not in verifier.c, which drops CAP_SYS_ADMIN, which
libbpf needs to find the kfuncs of bpf_testmod.
